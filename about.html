OUR HEART

Jesus at the centre.
People at heart.

RCCG Open Heavens Church exists to honour God, share the good news of Jesus Christ, and help people grow into a life of faith, purpose, and service.

We want every person who walks through our doors to feel welcomed, encouraged, and equipped to live out God's Word at home, at work, and in the community.
